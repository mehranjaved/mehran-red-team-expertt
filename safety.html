RESPONSIBLE SECURITY

Ethics & Safety

Technical ability must be matched with permission, care and accountability.

Authorization first

Practice only on systems you own, dedicated training labs or systems for which you have explicit permission and a clearly defined scope.

Protect privacy

Do not access, collect, publish or retain personal information that is not necessary and explicitly authorized.

Minimize impact

Avoid actions that disrupt services, change data, affect other users or go beyond the learning objective.

Report responsibly

Follow the owner's disclosure process. Share findings privately with enough context to support remediation.

Before any practical test

  • Is this a purpose-built training lab or do I have explicit permission?
  • Do I understand the permitted scope and rules?
  • Could this action affect another person, account, service or data?
  • Can I achieve the learning objective with a safer, isolated exercise?
  • Can I explain the defensive lesson and remediation?
